Vocal Shortcuts

Say your own phrase, such as “Over to Amber”, and it runs a Shortcut with no “Hey Siri” first. Audio is processed on the phone.

Settings › Accessibility › Vocal Shortcuts

Siri Pause Time

Default, Longer, or Longest, for how long Siri waits for you to finish. Longer stops it clipping names.

Settings › Accessibility › Siri

Back Tap

Double or triple tap the back of the phone to run a feature or Shortcut.

Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tapoverview

Accessibility Shortcut

Triple-click the side button to toggle the features you pick.

Settings › Accessibility › Accessibility Shortcut

3

Speak Screen, Speak Selection, typing feedback

Swipe down with two fingers to hear the page, or select text and tap Speak. Voice, language, and rate are set here too.

Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak

Live Speech

Type and the phone speaks it, in calls, FaceTime, or out loud; saves favorite phrases.

Settings › Accessibility › Live Speech

Live recognition and hearing

4

Live Recognition

The camera describes what’s around you: detect text and read it aloud, detect doors, people, and furniture, and set up Live Recognition shortcuts.

Magnifier app

Sound Recognition

Alerts for 15 sounds such as doorbells and sirens; can learn your name or appliances. Apple says not to rely on it in emergencies.

Settings › Accessibility › Sound Recognitionoverview

Live Captions

Captions any audio on the phone, including calls. iPhone 11 or later.

Settings › Accessibility › Live Captions

Music Haptics

Taps synced to songs in Apple Music, Shazam, and some other apps; Full Mix or Vocals Only, three strengths.

Settings › Accessibility › Music Haptics

Voice Control

Run the whole phone by voice (“tap Send”, numbered grids); works offline after a one-time download.

AssistiveTouch

An on-screen menu for gestures and buttons, Dwell Control, mouse or trackpad pointers, and actions triggered by sounds like a mouth pop.

Switch Control

Drive the phone with one or more switches, including Head Tracking with the front camera.
